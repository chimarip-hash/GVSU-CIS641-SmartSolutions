# Smart Solutions

Smart Solutions is developing a Life Counter App for Magic: The Gathering. The application allows two or more players to track their life points, add or subtract points, view their current scores, and reset their life totals. Players can be added or removed during a game, with a minimum of two players required. The goal is to make life-point tracking simple and accurate.

## Prerequisites

A web browser and an internet connection are needed to open the hosted website. No additional software is required.

## Run Instructions

Open the Life Counter website:

[https://chimarip-hash.github.io/GVSU-CIS641-SmartSolutions/life-counter.html](https://chimarip-hash.github.io/GVSU-CIS641-SmartSolutions/life-counter.html)

1. Enter a name for each player.
2. All players start with 20 life points.
3. Click **Add Player** to add another player.
4. Click **Remove Player** to remove a player. At least two players must remain.
5. Enter a positive whole number, such as 1 or 5.
6. Click **Add** or **Subtract** to change the selected player's life total.
7. Click **Reset** on a player's card to return that player's life total to 20.
8. Click **Reset All** to return every player's life total to 20. Player names stay the same.

No installation is needed. The website runs in a web browser on a computer or phone.

Refreshing or closing the page clears the current game.
